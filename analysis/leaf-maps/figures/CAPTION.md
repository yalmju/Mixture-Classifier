# 캡션 원문

## Fig_leaf_maps

**Raman maps of the four leaf-surface preparations.** Each panel is a 10 × 10 pixel map
(50 µm step, 450 × 450 µm) of one preparation, plotted after asymmetric-least-squares
baseline removal (λ = 1 × 10⁵, p = 0.01). **Top row**, integrated intensity of the ink
reporter band in the cellular-silent region (2100–2180 cm⁻¹), which reports the amount of
ink probed in each pixel. **Lower rows**, the marker-band intensity of diquat (DQ, 1179 /
1521 / 1576 cm⁻¹), thiabendazole (TBZ, 779 / 1011 / 1549 cm⁻¹) and thiophanate-methyl
(THI, 556 / 1138 / 1365 cm⁻¹), each averaged over the three bands (± 10 cm⁻¹) and divided
by the ink reporter intensity of the same pixel, so that pixel-to-pixel differences in
substrate enhancement cancel. The intensity scale is shared across the four preparations
within a row (limits given by the colour bar) but not between rows.

> 캡션에 성분 정식명을 넣을 때 프로젝트에서 쓰는 약어와 맞출 것. 위 문장의
> "thiophanate-methyl" 은 THI 의 자리표시자다 — 실제 화합물명으로 바꿔 쓸 것.

## Fig_leaf_spectra

**Representative spectra of the four maps.** (**a**) Median spectrum of the 100 pixels of
each map in the fingerprint region, normalised to the integrated ink reporter band
(2100–2180 cm⁻¹) of the same map and offset vertically for display; shading spans the
interquartile range of the 100 pixels within that one preparation. Vertical lines mark the
marker bands used in Fig_leaf_maps (colours as in the legend). (**b**) The same spectra in
the silent region, plotted as raw counts; the dashed line marks 2137 cm⁻¹. The reporter
band is present in every preparation, which is what makes the ratio readout in (a) and in
Fig_leaf_maps meaningful.

> 산포 표기 주의 — 음영은 **한 조제 안** 100 픽셀의 사분위 범위이지 독립 반복 100회가
> 아니다. 수식어 없는 `n = 100` 은 쓰지 말 것 (`analysis/dq9-sus-reproducibility/README.md`
> §3). 조제간 재현성 오차막대가 필요하면 같은 조건을 한 번 더 조제해야 한다.
